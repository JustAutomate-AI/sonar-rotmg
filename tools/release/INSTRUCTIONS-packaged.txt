SONAR - damage tracker for Realm of the Mad God
================================================

Sonar reads the game's network traffic on your own PC and shows who is dealing
how much damage in the instance you are in. It never changes, blocks or sends
anything. Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) only.


INSTALL
-------

1. Unpack the whole zip to a normal folder, for example Documents\Sonar.
   Do not run it from inside the zip, and avoid Program Files and OneDrive.

2. Double-click  Sonar.exe

   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then
   "Run anyway". This appears because the file was downloaded.

   That is the install. Java is not needed - Sonar brings its own.

3. The first time it opens it will offer to put a shortcut on your desktop
   and in the Start menu, and will say so if Npcap is missing.


NPCAP
-----

Npcap is the driver that lets Sonar see network packets. It is the one thing
Sonar cannot bring with it, because its licence does not allow that.

If Sonar says it is missing, use the link it offers, or get it from
https://npcap.com/#download and when the installer asks:

  - keep  "Install Npcap in WinPcap API-compatible Mode"  ticked
  - leave "Restrict Npcap driver's access to Administrators only" unticked

Everything except the sniffer works without it.


RUN
---

1. Start the game.
2. Open Sonar.
3. Press "Start sniffer" in the top right corner.
4. Enter a new area in the game, for example the Nexus. Sonar needs to see an
   area load once before it can follow the game.

The sniffer has to be started each time you open Sonar, and it stops when you
close Sonar. "Auto-start" in the left rail can do it for you.

Item and enemy names and pictures come from the game's own files, which Sonar
reads on first run. That takes a moment the first time and is then kept.

To keep Sonar on the taskbar, right-click its shortcut (on Windows 11 choose
"Show more options" first), or right-click the Sonar button on the taskbar
while it is running, and choose "Pin to taskbar".


USING IT
--------

Live          The damage list, your own numbers and the current fight.
              Overall / Enemy / Bosses   which damage is counted
              DPS / Total                damage per second or total damage dealt
              Everyone / Party           all players or only your party
              Click a player's bar to see what their damage came from.
              Click the rank number at the left end of a bar to add that
              player to your party or remove them.
              "Reset" (small, top right of the damage list) sets the damage
              shown for this instance to zero. It needs a second click to
              confirm, and the arrow that appears beside it undoes it.
              The two arrows beside the instance name step back through
              finished instances and forward again to the live one.
Players       Everyone who dealt damage. Pick one to see their damage by item,
              their hits, biggest hit and equipment. Your party is listed
              here too; click a member to remove them.
Instance      Details of the area, every enemy hit, finished instances and
              your character's eight stats. Click an enemy or a finished
              instance to see it on the Live page.
Analysis      Where a fight's damage went, boss by boss and wave by wave.
Vault         What is in your vault, gift chest and potion storage.
Characters    Your characters, their gear, stats and pets.
Exaltation    Exaltation progress per class.
Wardrobe      Skins and dyes, and how Sonar draws each class.
Collection    What you have found, and what is left.
Party finder  Players you have seen, and who you keep running with.
Fame          What a character is worth now and what it would be worth dead.
Puzzle        The Shatters observatory puzzle, solved from what the game sends.
Calculator    What an item or ability actually hits for.
Daily Quests  The day's quests, what they ask for and what they pay.

Windows can be dragged by their title, resized from the right and bottom edge
and closed with the X. "Windows" brings closed ones back and "Reset layout"
restores the defaults. Your layout and party are remembered.


IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK
--------------------------

  Nothing shows after starting the sniffer
      Turn off any VPN, run only one game client, and enter a new area.
  "You are missing a program called Npcap"
      Reinstall Npcap with "WinPcap API-compatible Mode" ticked.
  The sniffer stops with "Stream Constructor reached 50 packets"
      This is packet loss, usually Wi-Fi. Use a network cable if you can.
  Wrong or missing names after a game update
      Delete the "assets" folder beside Sonar.exe and open Sonar again; it
      reads the game's files afresh.
  Sonar does not open
      Check that the whole zip was unpacked, not just Sonar.exe. The "app"
      and "runtime" folders beside it are what it runs on.


UPDATING
--------

Sonar looks for a newer version each time it starts, and "Check for updates"
at the bottom of the left rail does the same on demand. When there is one,
choose "Update and restart": Sonar downloads it, closes, installs it and
starts again. Your layout, party and settings are kept.

If the automatic update fails, download the new zip from the release page and
unpack it over this folder.


UNINSTALL
---------

Delete this folder, the desktop shortcut and the Sonar entry in the Start menu
(right-click it > Open file location > delete). Unpin it from the taskbar if
you pinned it. Npcap is a separate program and can be removed from Windows
Settings > Apps if you no longer want it.
